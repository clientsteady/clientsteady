The Problem

A bad diagnosis creates a bad recommendation — even when the reporting is accurate.

When performance moves, agencies form a diagnosis and send a recommendation. The quality of that diagnosis — and the explanation behind it — determines whether the client stays or escalates.

Failure mode 01
The wrong diagnosis
CPL is up, so the media must be inefficient. But sessions were up too, and CPCs barely moved. The break was post-click — and the team never checked.
Failure mode 02
The wrong recommendation
Cut spend and tighten targeting. Logical, defensible, and exactly wrong — because it treats the symptom while the actual cause continues untouched.
Failure mode 03
The client conversation
The account manager goes in with a metric summary instead of a diagnosis. The client pushes back. The founder has to step in again — or doesn't, and loses the account.
The risk is not just a bad metric. It is acting on the wrong explanation. When the diagnosis is weak, the recommendation can be perfectly logical and still move the account in the wrong direction — and the client conversation becomes one the agency cannot win.
