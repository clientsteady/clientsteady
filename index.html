FAQ

Common questions.

Do I need to replace my existing booking software?
No — ClientSteady layers on top of whatever you're already using. We connect to your calendar via integration or a simple data sync. If you don't have a booking system yet, we can set one up for you as part of onboarding.
How does the SMS actually get sent? Do I need a phone number?
We provision a dedicated business number for your account during setup. All messages come from that number — your personal number is never exposed. Clients can reply and the system handles responses automatically.
How long does setup take?
Most businesses are live within 5 business days. We handle all the technical setup — you answer a few questions about your services and message tone, we build the flows, and you review and approve every message before anything goes live.
What if my clients don't respond to texts?
SMS has a 98% open rate vs 20% for email. SMS typically sees significantly higher engagement than email for appointment confirmations. The system also works via email as a fallback for clients who opt out of SMS.
Can I customize the messages to match my brand voice?
Yes — every message template is customizable during onboarding. On Growth and Enterprise plans, AI personalizes each message based on the client's name, service, and history. You set the tone; the AI handles the variation.
What industries do you support right now?
We're live with med spas and aesthetic practices. Salons, dental offices, chiropractors, and home services are in active development. If your business runs on appointments and repeat clients, reach out — we onboard new verticals regularly.
