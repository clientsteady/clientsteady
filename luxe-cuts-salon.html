About Us

Salon artistry
with a personal touch

Luxe Cuts & Color was founded by master stylist Camille Renner, a Vidal Sassoon-trained colorist with 18 years behind the chair. Our Southlake studio is built on one belief: great hair should feel effortless.

Every new client starts with a complimentary style consultation. We listen to your goals, assess your hair, and build a plan — never a one-size-fits-all approach.
