Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

1. Who We Are

ClientSteady ("we," "us," or "our") is an automated SMS client retention platform for service businesses, operated by Justin Bishop out of Dallas-Fort Worth, Texas. You can reach us at hello@clientsteady.com.

2. Information We Collect

When you interact with ClientSteady — including submitting our contact form, requesting a demo, or signing up as a client — we may collect:

  • Your name, email address, and phone number
  • Your business name, city, and website
  • Information about your booking platform and business operations
  • Usage data collected automatically through Google Analytics (GA4)
  • Phone numbers entered in our demo booking forms

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to inquiries and schedule demos
  • To set up and deliver our SMS automation services
  • To send you transactional communications about your account
  • To improve our website and service offerings
  • To comply with legal obligations

4. SMS and Phone Data

ClientSteady's core service delivers automated SMS messages on behalf of our business clients to their end customers. Our demo sites use simulated SMS flows and do not send real text messages. If you provide a phone number as part of onboarding, that number may be used to send automated SMS messages via Twilio. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message. We do not sell or share phone numbers with third parties for marketing purposes.

5. Third-Party Processors

We use the following third-party services to operate our business:

  • Netlify — website hosting
  • Twilio — SMS delivery
  • Google Analytics (GA4) — website analytics
  • HubSpot — CRM and email logging
  • Stripe — payment processing
  • Zoho Mail — email communications

Each of these services has its own privacy policy governing their use of data.

6. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as necessary to provide our services or as required by law. You may request deletion of your data at any time by emailing hello@clientsteady.com.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you. To exercise these rights, contact us at hello@clientsteady.com.

8. Cookies

We use cookies through Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our website. You can disable cookies in your browser settings. We do not use cookies for advertising.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post any changes on this page with an updated date. Continued use of our services after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy? Contact us at hello@clientsteady.com
